Single-threaded numpy; the work is dense float64 reductions over (chunk, |H|, |O|).

the chunk size: 5,000 items x 100 hypotheses x 6 options x 8 bytes x 2 = 46 MiB on
the `size` tile. T2 can raise the chunk freely; cost is linear.

batch job. A pure-Python per-item loop over the same pool was not implemented;
the vectorised path was written first, per T1 requirement 2.

### K1 note

The complement of `robust` is K1's gate statistic `|D(infinity)| / N` on the item
base preregistration §7.1 specifies, so this benchmark computed the gate number as
a byproduct: pooled `0.1823`, grid rate `|D(8)|/N = 0.1795`, outcome 2, pass. That
is disclosed with its ordering in `docs/P2/PREREGISTRATION_v2.1.md` §1. It is not
an Arm A result, and T6 Step 1 still runs it as a reproduction check.

`robust` is the share of items with `beta_c = inf`, i.e. where `o*_0` already
maximises margin and no finite persuasion budget moves the optimum. It falls
monotonically with `|O|` (96.1% at `|O|=3` down to 66.6% at `|O|=6`), which is
the `|O|` gradient spec Section 7.2 flags as a confound for T6's kill gate. This
is a T1 observation on the *candidate pool*, not a T6 result: T6's gate is
defined on the T2 redraw, and this number is not that number.
