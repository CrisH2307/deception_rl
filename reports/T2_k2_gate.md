## What is driving the number

The two tie blocks are very nearly the same set of items:

| conditioned on | N | share adversary-robust (`beta_c = inf`) | median finite `beta_c` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `fit_cost == 0` (non-conflict) | 162,694 | 98.29% | 5.7890 |
| `fit_cost > 0` (conflict) | 37,306 | 9.71% | 3.3767 |

Both variables are, structurally, the same kind of statement: `fit_cost = 0` says P1's fit-maximizing option is already the posterior-maximizing option, and `beta_c = infinity` says the posterior-maximizing option is already the margin-maximizing option. On this pool those two coincidences co-occur, and the conditional rates above are the whole of the pooled rank correlation. Items where the Scientist gives up nothing by choosing rationally are also the items the adversary cannot move, and the sign is negative because among the remainder a larger `fit_cost` goes with a smaller budget needed to flip the optimum.

**Cross-check on `beta_c` itself.** The existence rate implied here, `|D(infinity)| / N = 0.1823`, reproduces the value T1's benchmark computed independently on the same pool (0.1823, `reports/T1_benchmark.md`, disclosed as K1's statistic in `docs/P2/PREREGISTRATION_v2.1.md` section 1.2). The gate statistic is not resting on a fresh, unchecked `beta_c` run.
