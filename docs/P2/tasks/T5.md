## Note added 2026-09-09 by the T6 session. An open question, routed here, not decided there.

T6 measured something that bears on Arm B's design. **This note decides nothing.**
The session that produced the numbers is not the session that may set a rule on
them, and this is written so the ordering is on the record before T5 rules.

### The measurement

`o*_infinity` is Paper 1's `o_fit` on **452 of 460** divergent items in the frozen
set and on **0.9246** of the pool's 36,464 (`reports/T6_arm_a.md` Step 3,
`results/T6_arm_a_numbers.json`). Arm B's target therefore sits **on Paper 1's
salience pole**. Paper 1's headline is that models sit outside the salience-Bayes
interval on the far side of salience.

### The concern this raised, and how far it goes

Two consequences, and only one of them was ever a threat.

1. **The LEVEL of `A` is confounded, and this is not fixable and not a defect.**
   Sitting at `o*_infinity` is not evidence of adversary tracking; it is the
   salience-driven behaviour Paper 1 documented. Any claim resting on raw `A` under
   a single framing inherits that confound.
2. **The CONTRASTS are not confounded.** Salience-driven behaviour is constant
   across framings, cancels in `F1 - F0` and `F2 - F0`, and yields `ΔA = 0`. v2.0
   section 4 already makes the primary measure a contrast, so the design survives
   this. What was at risk was **power**: if models sat at or past `A = 1` under F0
   there would be no headroom, and the preregistered "little or no movement"
   prediction would be confirmed by a ceiling. A prediction confirmed by a ceiling
   carries no evidence.

### The power question was measured, and the answer is that the ceiling is not there

`reports/T6_F0_headroom.md`, `results/T6_F0_headroom.json`. F0 is P1's `cond4`, so
this read frozen P1 output and ran no model. On the 460-item divergence set,
`pmi` / `template`:

| model | `A >= 1` | median `A` | median headroom | `size` tile `A >= 1` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `CTRL` | 0.0630 | -0.8943 | +1.8943 | 0.0278 |
| `B2` | 0.0630 | +0.0000 | +1.0000 | 0.2407 |
| `B4` | 0.1870 | +0.0000 | +1.0000 | 0.0463 |
| `L1` | 0.0217 | -0.6152 | +1.6152 | 0.0648 |
| `L2` | 0.0174 | -0.5096 | +1.5096 | 0.0278 |
| `L3` | 0.0304 | -0.5228 | +1.5228 | 0.0463 |
| `L4` | 0.1543 | -0.3940 | +1.3940 | 0.1019 |

Every model has room. Median `A` is at or below zero for all seven, and median
headroom runs 1.0 to 1.9. **A null on `ΔA` would not be produced by saturation,
because there is no saturation to produce it.**

Two things a reader of that table needs:

- **`A > 1` is unrepresentable.** `A` is bounded above by 1 (v2.0 section 3.2,
  `marg_norm <= 1`), verified at a maximum of exactly 1.0 over every option of every
  divergent item. "Strictly past the target" cannot be asked on this axis, so its
  zero count is structural and is not evidence about any model.
- **Asked on Paper 1's own coordinate, where it is representable, about half of each
  model's choices sit at or past the salience pole** (`sb <= 0` on 0.4875 to 0.5786),
  almost all of it strictly past. That reproduces P1's finding under F0. It does not
  put models at Arm B's target, because `A` is the margin axis and a far-side option
  has a low posterior and a low margin. The two coordinates order options
  differently; both tables are in the report.

### What T5 is being asked to rule on

Nothing here forces a change. The options, stated without a recommendation:

1. Restrict Arm B's primary analysis to items with F0 headroom. Note that this is
   selection on an F0-measured quantity and would need its own justification against
   `v2.2` section 1.3's prohibition.
2. Add a second coordinate not confounded with salience at the level.
3. Accept it and state the limitation on what a null can mean, given that the
   measured headroom is large and the contrast already cancels the confound.

**Option 3 is what the numbers as measured support, and saying so is not choosing
it.** T5 decides, with the ordering disclosed: the numbers above existed before any
rule was proposed, and no rule was proposed in the session that produced them.

### One implementation hazard, unrelated to the above, for whoever writes T7

v2.0 section 3.2 defines `A`'s domain by "`ext_i > 0` if and only if
`beta_c(i) < infinity`". The equivalence holds mathematically. In float it fails on
**one** item of the frozen 1,000: a rival beats `o*_0`'s margin by less than P1's
D51 tie-break tolerance, so the tie-break keeps `o*_0` and `beta_c` is infinite,
while `1 - marg_norm(o*_0)` comes out at `2.2e-16` instead of 0. An implementation
taking `A`'s domain from the sign of `ext_i` divides by that and hands Arm B a
finite `A` of order `1e16` for an adversary-robust item. Section 6's
`ext >= 0.02` floor does not catch it if the floor is applied after `A` is formed.
**Take the domain from `beta_c`.** The smallest genuine `ext_i` on the divergence
set is `2.09e-04`, four orders of magnitude clear, so the two cases separate
cleanly. `src/t6_f0_headroom.py` does this and its `--demo` asserts it.
