# T2: Item redraw and beta_c stratification (Wave 1)

Execute the task below. Obey `CLAUDE.md` invariants. If any instruction here
conflicts with `CLAUDE.md`, stop and report the conflict rather than choosing.

---

You are designing the item sample for Paper 2 of a research program.

CONTEXT
Paper 1 drew 1,000 items from a pool of roughly 200,000 candidates, stratified on a
scalar called fit_cost. Paper 2 needs a fresh draw from the SAME pool, stratified
instead on beta_c, the critical adversary budget at which the optimal signal flips.
Items with beta_c = infinity are adversary-robust and serve as built-in controls.

Rationale for redrawing rather than reusing: outside the divergence set the
no-adversary and adversary-aware oracles predict the same signal, so those items
carry no discriminating power for the model experiment. Stratifying on beta_c
concentrates the sample where the manipulation can be detected. Both draws come from
the same pool, so the Paper 1 comparison is preserved.

INPUTS
- Spec: <<REPO>>/docs/spec/adversary-game-v1.md
- Pool construction code from Paper 1 at <<PATH_TO_POOL>>. The pool, vocabulary, fit
  function, and rendering format are FROZEN. You change only the stratification
  variable.
- beta_c values come from T1's beta_critical(). Write your code against the contract
  in spec section 6. Do NOT write your own beta_c implementation; if T1 is not ready,
  stub the call and mark the stub clearly.

TASK

STEP 1 IS THE K2 GATE. COMPUTE AND REPORT IT BEFORE DESIGNING ANYTHING.

1. Compute K2 FIRST, before the stratification design, and stop on it if it fires.

   K2, from preregistration section 7.1: Spearman rank correlation of beta_c
   against fit_cost over the full 200,000-candidate pool, with beta_c = infinity
   ranked at the maximum, tied at the top. If |rho| >= 0.80 the redraw rationale
   collapses: beta_c and fit_cost are the same variable in disguise, stratifying
   on one is stratifying on the other, and Paper 2's item set would not differ
   from Paper 1's in the way the design claims. Report the number and STOP. Do
   not design bins, do not draw a sample, do not proceed to step 2. Report the
   Pearson correlation on the finite subset as a secondary descriptive number,
   not thresholded.

   Why this moved to the front (added 2026-09-09, after T1). K2 is more likely to
   fire than it appeared when T5 set it. T1 found that between-tile variation in
   the existence rate is not explained by |O|: two tiles with identical |O| = 3
   differ by fourteen points. The leading alternative explanation is each tile's
   rating spread relative to its bin width, which is conceptually adjacent to
   what fit_cost measures. That raises the prior on beta_c and fit_cost sharing
   structure. A gate that is plausibly live must be computed before the work it
   gates, not reported alongside it, because a number produced after a
   stratification design exists is a number someone will want to argue with.

   This is a reordering, not a threshold change. The 0.80 and the
   Spearman-with-infinity-ranked-top operationalization are exactly as
   preregistered.

2. Then characterize the beta_c distribution over the full pool: shape, mass at
   infinity, and the fit_cost relationship in full (the K2 statistic from step 1
   plus the scatter and any non-monotone structure a rank correlation hides).

3. Design the stratification: bin edges over finite beta_c, quota for the
   beta_c = infinity control stratum, target n per cell. Justify bin edges against
   the distribution rather than picking round numbers.

   SAMPLE-SIZE TARGET, from the preregistration (docs/P2/PREREGISTRATION_v2.md
   section 8.2): the draw must deliver AT LEAST 400 size-tile items with finite
   beta_c. Paper 1's primary set was 125 size-tile conflict items, so this is a
   materially larger requirement and it is stated before you fix quotas rather
   than discovered when Arm B turns out underpowered.

   Where 400 comes from. Arm B's confirmatory analysis runs on size-tile items
   with finite beta_c (preregistration section 2 inherits Paper 1's D49 and D108:
   only the size tile supports the task). Scoring is deterministic, so all
   uncertainty is item-level and the paired test needs
   n = 15.05 * sigma^2 / delta^2 at the Bonferroni-corrected alpha = 0.05/21 and
   80% power, with delta = 0.05 the SESOI carried over from Paper 1's D74:

     sigma (per-item SD of Delta-A)   0.10  0.15  0.20  0.25  0.30  0.40
     items needed                       61   136   241   377   542   964

   400 covers sigma up to 0.25.

   THE TARGET IS PROVISIONAL. sigma has never been measured, for anything: no P2
   data exists, and Delta-A is a new coordinate, so the table above is a curve over
   an assumption rather than a computed sample size. sigma becomes estimable once
   Arm A supplies beta_c and the F0 condition is scored, and the preregistration
   commits to reporting realized power at that point. If Arm A's variance estimate
   moves sigma materially, the target is revised then; do not treat 400 as a
   measured requirement, and do not treat a draw that clears it as evidence the
   study is powered.

   The beta_c = infinity control stratum is drawn from the size tile as well, so
   the control and analysis sets are comparable on |O|.

   If the pool cannot supply 400 such items, that is itself a finding about the
   signal space. Report it prominently next to the beta_c characterization; do not
   relax the target to fit what the pool has.
